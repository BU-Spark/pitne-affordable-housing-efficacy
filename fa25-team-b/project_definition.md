# Q2 Project Definition
Our project partners with the Citizens’ Housing and Planning Association (CHAPA), a non-profit focused on affordable housing in Massachusetts. The goal is to analyze application data for affordable homeownership opportunities across the state to understand applicant movement patterns, geographic trends, and demographic representation.
Despite previous analyses, key questions remain around where applicants live compared to where they apply, how demographic groups differ in application patterns, and the effect of CHAPA’s limited and age-restricted property portfolio on applicant diversity. This project aims to uncover insights that inform policies to increase equity and access to affordable housing.
We will use a combination of datasets, mainly:
 Structured Affordable Housing Application Dataset (2023-2025): Detailed recent application data including demographics, income, household info, and housing preferences.
Fragmented Historical Data (2021-2023): Older, incomplete data to provide additional historical perspectives.
Additional Recent Data (Mid-2025): Four months of new application data from recent lotteries, to be merged into the main dataset.
Census & Geographic Data: To facilitate geospatial analysis of applicant origins and application sites.
Potential integration of external datasets (e.g., Boston affordable housing applications) if obtained later.
## Project Objectives
1. Analyze geographic movement: Determine distances applicants travel to apply, comparing by race, age, household type, and other demographics.
2. Investigate portfolio effects: Assess whether the applicant pool is shaped by CHAPA’s mostly suburban, age-restricted properties.
3. Understand the impact of housing price and application complexity on applicant demographics and quantity.
4. Provide detailed visualizations (5-7) and narrative insights to inform CHAPA’s policy and outreach strategies.
5. Deliver clean code, documented cleaning procedures, a final report, and a presentation communicating findings clearly to stakeholders.
